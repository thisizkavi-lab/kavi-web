## Why this exists

Rather than presenting AlphaFold as a black box, this project breaks the idea into small, inspectable experiments: representations, pairwise information, geometric updates, and structure evaluation.

The first milestone is deliberately modest: a toy model that can be trained, visualized, and tested on a small public dataset. The goal is understanding and reproducibility—not claiming parity with AlphaFold.

## Status

Work in progress. The implementation will be published with notes on what is simplified, what is borrowed from prior work, and where the toy model fails.
