The Idea of Self

5 January 2025

I have come to see the self as three things tangled together: body, mind, and spirit. I dislike separating them, because whenever one slips, the other two eventually feel it. The body is the simplest part and the hardest to argue with. Underneath all the noise around health, it comes down to food, movement, and rest. When I keep the vessel running, the mind stays clearer. The mind holds memory, identity, and whatever craft I have built, though I keep noticing how easily it spins up static and loops even when everything else is fine.

Spirit turned out to be simpler than I once thought. To me, it is the sense of moving in a direction that makes me genuinely excited to wake up and work. I do not know whether a soul exists, yet the idea still works for me. With that pull, even pain makes sense; without it, both body and mind slowly dull. Direction never came to me from sitting around arguing with myself. It only started showing up through friction, reflection, and staying with hard work.

None of this happens alone. I live among other selves, and who I am keeps getting shaped in the back-and-forth with them. Saying yes to a dinner or a friend’s invitation sometimes costs me my routine, yet other people are also what keep me from drifting too far into my own head. Seeing myself and others clearly, without turning arrogant and without losing myself trying to fit everyone else, has no clean answer. Life stays messy. I do not think I ever settle it once. I just keep navigating… living… until death, I guess.
