## Protein dynamics with deep learning

I am studying biophysics and protein dynamics as a graduate student in the **Shoji Takada Lab** at Kyoto University.

My current direction is to understand how proteins move between functional states, and how machine-learning models can describe those motions while staying connected to physical evidence. The work is still taking shape: the systems, data, baselines, and evaluation criteria should become more specific as the research develops.

This page will grow with the research. For now, it is a clear starting point rather than a list of finished publications or claims.
