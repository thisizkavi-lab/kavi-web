I'm a graduate student at [Kyoto University](https://www.kyoto-u.ac.jp/en). I work on understanding how proteins move and change shape over time.

We've become very good at determining the 3D structures of proteins, but structure alone doesn't tell us how they behave. Inside cells, proteins are constantly moving and shifting between different conformations.

My research focuses on **protein dynamics**—how proteins move, switch between states, and how those motions enable them to function. The problem is inherently interdisciplinary and draws on both mechanistic, physics-based models and deep-learning methods to understand protein motion.

Understanding these motions can also help us understand how proteins interact with other molecules, with applications in areas such as drug discovery.
